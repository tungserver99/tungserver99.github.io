01 About

Research that connects strong methods with practical deployment.

I am an AI researcher and educator with a background in machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing. I received a B.Eng. in Computer Engineering and an M.Sc. in Data Science from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

My current work spans neural topic modeling, large language models, model compression, continual learning, and knowledge distillation, with an emphasis on efficiency, robustness, and interpretable learning.
