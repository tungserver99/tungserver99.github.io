ABOUT

Research at the intersection of NLP, efficient AI, and trustworthy learning.

I am an AI researcher and educator with a background in machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing. I received a B.Eng. in Computer Engineering and an M.Sc. in Data Science from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

My work spans neural topic modeling, large language models, model compression, continual learning, and knowledge distillation. I am particularly interested in methods that retain strong model quality while reducing computational cost and improving robustness in practical deployment settings.
