About Me

I am a PhD candidate working jointly with the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) and Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with current interests in efficient and trustworthy large language models, neural topic modeling, and knowledge distillation.

I received my B.Eng. in Computer Engineering and M.Sc. in Data Science from HUST. Alongside research, I have experience in university teaching and industrial AI development, including speech recognition, NLP systems, retrieval-augmented generation, and production machine learning.

Research Interests

  • Efficient and Trustworthy Large Language Models: post-training quantization, robustness, model ownership, and efficient deployment.
  • Neural Topic Modeling: interpretable topic discovery, short-text modeling, optimal transport, contrastive learning, and LLM-assisted topic modeling.
  • Knowledge Distillation: transferring knowledge across models with different tokenizers, vocabularies, and representation spaces.
